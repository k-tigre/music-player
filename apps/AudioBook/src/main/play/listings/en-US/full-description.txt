An offline audiobook player for files already on your phone. Add a folder of books, pick up where you left off, and listen without streaming — including at bedtime.

What the app does:
• Library from a folder on your device (mp3, m4b, and other audio files)
• Chapters with saved playback position
• Seek ±15s / ±1 min
• Playback speed control
• Sleep timer with smooth volume fade-out
• Extend the timer by shaking or flipping the phone
• Equalizer
• Home screen widgets
• Android Auto: browse books and play
• Background playback

No account and no streaming — just your files. Core listening is free; equalizer, extra sleep-timer options, and the home widget are available in Plus and Pro.
