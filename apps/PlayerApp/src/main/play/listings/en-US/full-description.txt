Music Player is a free offline music player for your local library. No accounts, no subscriptions, no streaming — just the audio on your phone, with background playback and a clear library.

Why people use it:
• Browse by Artists → Albums → Tracks
• Persistent queue that remembers your list between sessions
• Playlists and favorites for tracks and albums
• Full-screen player and mini-player while you multitask
• Built-in equalizer for headphones or speakers
• Android Auto: library, search, and queue in the car
• Home screen widgets for quick play/pause and skip
• Open mp3 and other audio files from Downloads, Telegram, and other apps

A simple local music player that stays private: your files stay on the device, and nothing requires a sign-up.
